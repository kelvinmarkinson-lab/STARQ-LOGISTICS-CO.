About STARQ LOGISTICS & CO.

Dedicated air cargo shipping and logistics solutions operating from Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria — connecting commercial e-commerce across domestic and international skies.

Company Background

Built Around the Demands of Modern Air Freight

STARQ LOGISTICS & CO. is an air cargo shipping company positioned in Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria. Our business is focused on providing structured, professional air freight transport for commercial enterprises and e-commerce merchants.

In today's fast-moving retail landscape, businesses require predictable cargo handling and transparent shipping arrangements. We serve companies that need to move products quickly across Nigeria or coordinate cross-border air freight to international destinations.

By operating directly from Ikeja — the primary commercial aviation district of Lagos — we provide a practical, business-focused gateway for companies seeking clear quote assessments, careful package handling, and responsive customer service.

Core Principles

Our Mission & Company Values

We believe commercial logistics should be transparent, straightforward, and built on realistic, dependable service standards.

Our Stated Mission

"To deliver reliable, accessible, and transparent air cargo shipping solutions that empower e-commerce businesses and commercial shippers to connect across Nigeria and international markets with total clarity."

We focus on doing what we do with precision: dedicated air freight transport, clear quote evaluations, and dedicated shipper communication.

Reliability & Accountability

Transparent Quotations

Operational Clarity

Strategic Advantage

Operating From Ikeja, Lagos

Ikeja is the administrative center and the undisputed aviation capital of Lagos State, situated in immediate proximity to Murtala Muhammed Airport's cargo operations.

This strategic geographic position allows STARQ LOGISTICS & CO. to provide swift coordination for cargo arriving from or departing to regional hubs across Nigeria, as well as air freight scheduled for international transport.

  • Direct access to Nigeria's primary domestic flight network
  • Access to international cargo carriers and scheduled freighters
  • Centralized handling for Lagos commercial e-commerce businesses
