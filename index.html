How It Works

Getting your air cargo moving with STARQ LOGISTICS & CO. is straightforward, transparent, and structured in three clear steps.

1

Submit Your Shipment Details

Provide your shipment origin, destination, cargo nature, weight in kilograms, and optional dimensions through our online quote request form.

Complete details allow our logistics coordinators to identify the most suitable air freight route.
2

Receive a Shipping Quote

Our team reviews your inquiry, validates weight and route specifications, and issues a detailed shipping quotation tailored to your timeline.

Quotes clearly outline routing, cargo handling parameters, and estimated flight schedules.
3

Confirm Your Shipment

Review and approve your shipping quote. Once confirmed, finalize cargo drop-off or pickup arrangements and documentation for air freight dispatch.

Arrangements and final scheduling are verified directly with you before flight booking.
