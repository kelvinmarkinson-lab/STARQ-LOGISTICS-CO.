Preparation Guidelines

Preparing Your Air Cargo for Transit

Proper packaging and accurate cargo declarations ensure efficient quote generation, swift handling, and smooth air transport.

Sturdy Outer Packaging

Use robust double-walled corrugated cartons or wooden crates suitable for aviation handling. Ensure cartons are securely taped along all seams.

Accurate Weight & Dimensions

Weigh each carton in kilograms and measure Length × Width × Height in centimeters. Accurate data ensures your quote is exact.

Clear Item Descriptions

Provide a specific description of contents (e.g., "Men's Cotton Shirts" rather than "General Goods"). Air aviation regulations require specific manifests.
